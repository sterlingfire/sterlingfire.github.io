I design the hardware that makes light move, and I help build the big pieces it lives in.
At Flowtoys I led 3D design and printing for LED props — the staves, connectors, and
housings that performers throw, drop, and beat against the ground all night. Every part I
drew, I took out and spun myself before it shipped. On the large-scale side, I've wired and
built the LED arches of Sonic Runway and designed the lighting for the Canopy.
